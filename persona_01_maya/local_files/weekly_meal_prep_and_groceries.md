# Weekly Meal Prep & Dietary Guidelines

**Dietary Preferences:** High-protein, low-inflammatory, lactose-conscious  
**Primary Supermarket:** Trader Joe's (University Ave) & Campus Farmer's Market  
**Usual Shopping Slot:** Sunday mornings (~11:00 AM) with Chloe  

### Weekly Meal Rotation
- **Breakfast:** Greek yogurt alternative with chia seeds, blueberries, and walnuts. Black cold brew coffee.
- **Lunch (Campus):** Meal-prepped grilled lemon herb chicken breast with roasted sweet potatoes, quinoa, and steamed broccoli.
- **Dinner:** Tofu stir-fry or baked salmon with brown rice and bok choy.
- **Pre-Gym Snack:** Banana with almond butter before 7:30 AM workouts.

### Recurring Shopping List
- [ ] Chicken breast (2 lbs organic)
- [ ] Firm organic tofu (2 blocks)
- [ ] Fresh Atlantic salmon fillets (1 lb)
- [ ] Sweet potatoes & baby spinach
- [ ] Blueberries & Honeycrisp apples
- [ ] Oat milk & unsweetened almond butter
- [ ] Sparkling water (lime/lemon 12-pack)
