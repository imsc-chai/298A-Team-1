# CarePortal UX Usability Research Synthesis (Round 2)

**Client:** HorizonHealth Technologies  
**Lead UX Researcher:** Elena Gomez  
**Sample:** 5 Telehealth Patients (Ages 28–64)  

### Key Takeaways
1. **Appointment Selection Friction:** 4 out of 5 users struggled to differentiate between video and in-person slots in the old modal. The new segmented pill switch reduced booking time from 94 seconds to 38 seconds.
2. **Medication Refill Flow:** Users praised the one-click prescription renewal toggle.
3. **Contrast Rating:** Dark mode contrast passed all accessibility audits with a 7.2:1 contrast ratio.
