# AP Biology Unit 4 Examination Scoring Rubric

**Unit:** Cellular Energetics & Respiration  
**Total Points:** 80 (40 Multiple Choice + 4 Free Response Questions)  

### Free Response Scoring Guidelines
- **FRQ 1 (10 pts):** Mitochondrial electron transport chain and proton gradient establishment.
- **FRQ 2 (10 pts):** Glycolysis ATP net yield under anaerobic vs. aerobic pathways.
- **FRQ 3 (10 pts):** Spectrophotometer absorbance curve graph analysis and standard error calculation.
- **FRQ 4 (10 pts):** Experimental design for yeast fermentation rates under temperature variants.
